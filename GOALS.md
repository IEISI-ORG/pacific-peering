
# Pacific Peering

- a project to capture every active ASPATH across the ASNs homed into Melanesia, Polynesia and Micronesia.
- excludes Hawaii but includes Guam.  Coverage is all economies covered by APNIC in the Oceania region excluding Australia and New Zealand.
- analysis will concentrate on discovered peering relationships, IXP routing and sub-optimal transpacific routing.
- RIPE ATLAS probes will be used to record full traceroutes between ASNs across these subregions.
- public RIS data will be used to build a "fish bowl" look into the routes across the Pacific.
- detective work will be a process of triangulation between sources and destinations of routes found.
